## HAVE — already covered
- **4, budgets and memory.** Scheduled campaigns carry `daily_budget` with an `enforce_budget` switch that stops a run for the day (`pan-wizard-core/bin/lib/campaign.cjs:81-118`, SOURCE). Every harness model step is refused without `--max-usd` (ADR-0047 D2). PAN's memory is the cited, expiring entries of MI-093 (`memory.cjs:156-179`, SOURCE). Cohere's per-user and per-group rate tiers have no PAN counterpart because the host makes the calls.
- **10, positioning.** The survey is n=166, self-selected, with no margin of error, so it is secondary by nature. Its top reasons are flexibility across models and tools (22%), production reliability (20%), ease of development (19%) and control over execution (18%). That matches PAN's position as a runtime-neutral layer with deterministic gates, and the August read that the market converged on PAN's thesis. Use it as colour, not as a claim.
- **11b, model-managed context through a file.**
  - Before the host compacts, the context monitor asks the model to checkpoint its position into `state.md` (`hooks/pan-context-monitor.js:32-40`, `:411-418`, SOURCE).
  - After compaction, the position is re-injected (`hooks/pan-state-reinject.js`, MI-036).
  - PAN's CLAUDE.md block steers the host's summary with compact instructions (`pan-wizard-core/bin/lib/agents-md.cjs:101`, MI-097).

  The paper's own failure mode supports the line PAN drew: an editable context lets a model plant instructions for itself, which is why PAN keeps the checkpoint to position fields.

## WATCH
- **5, SIFT.** A judge pays off when there are many candidates to rank. PAN's optimiser applies are rare (one apply run in 14 field projects, MI-061), and a harness A/B compares one or two variants. ADR-0029's scope fence keeps model judges out of lifecycle gates anyway. Trigger: MI-061's (`optimize stats` shows apply runs in three or more projects), or PAN generating several candidate variants per decision. If it fires, note the paper's finding that full files ranked better than diffs as judge input (Spearman 0.68 vs 0.40 over 50 nodes).
- **7, WikiSkill.** The gains are on math, QA, spreadsheet, office and ALFWorld tasks with small and mid-size models, with skills injected whole into the system prompt. PAN measured lessons on its own coding chain and found no effect (MI-095). It also declined skill preload into agents (ADR-0046 D4). The paper also reports that skills compiled for one model can hurt another (Qwen-4B skills cut Gemini's spreadsheet score from 50.5 to 18.1). That is a caution for anything PAN ships to every model. Trigger: MI-095's (a long-horizon harness run showing an effect), or a harness A/B where a compiled skill changes a fast-tier agent's outcome.
- **9a, Agent Memory Repo.** Published by Cognition on `2026-10-04`: MIT licence, no version or releases, no named adopter. It installs into other tools as a skill, not as host-native support. MI-010's trigger needs adoption by a runtime PAN targets, so it has not fired; the decline holds. The format is close to PAN's own: a `MEMORY.md` index, files read on demand, and bullet entries with `source` and `added` metadata. It has no expiry, which MI-093's entries do.

## How far to trust the material
- **The newsletter itself** is secondary. Every item above was checked against its primary source except EmbeddingGemma 2 (search snippets only) and the survey (VentureBeat is its own source). One headline is wrong: Mistral Large 4 has 52B active parameters per Mistral's docs, not 49B. The October 27 weights date appears only in secondary coverage.
- **Hugging Face multi-harness RL** used one seed per run, no error bars and a single attempt per test cell (250 tasks × 4 harnesses). The authors call the gap between their two RL runs noise. The 29-point spread between harnesses is large next to that, but it comes from one 2.6B model on data-analysis tasks. A frontier model's spread may be smaller, which is why the recommendation is to measure rather than assume.
- **SIFT** reports one main run plus 3 repeats for its headline, with no confidence intervals. Its strongest judge is stronger than the agent being improved, and two of its cost figures disagree with each other.
- **WikiSkill** averages 3 runs per result with a paired bootstrap. Its test sets are small (85–280 items) and its validation sets smaller still.
- **Context Language Models** report no error bars on the main results. Several gains are within plausible run-to-run noise, the FLOPs are theoretical, and the headline 11.4% is relative (about 6 points absolute).
- **Cognition**: no announcement post was found. The facts come from the spec page, the repository and the Devin docs.
