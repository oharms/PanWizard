## What not to do (carried from August, plus three new)

- Do not retire the markdown workflows; do not chase a sixth runtime; do not standardise `.planning/`; do not soften PanLoop; do not list on a skills marketplace on quality grounds alone.
- **Do not fork the skills compiler for the Agent Plugins bundle.** One converter, two call sites — the same rule that governs the MCP bridge.
- **Do not emit frontmatter the target runtime has not been seen to read.** ADR-0028's rule; `experimental.cacheTtl` and `maxTurns` are documented, but confirm on a live build before default-on.
- **Do not add a model rate without a primary pricing citation in the commit.** The Sonnet 5 row is what happens when a pre-announced price is written down and the announcement changes.
